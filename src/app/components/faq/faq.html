How should I light my candle for the first time? For how long should I let it burn?
For the first burn, always let the candle burn long enough (1.5 to 2 hours) so that the wax melts to the edges of the container. This prevents tunnelling and ensures an even burn in future uses. Always let your candle burn to the edge. Do not burn your candle for more than 3-4 hours at a time. Let the candle cool completely before touching or moving it to avoid spillage or burns.
How do I care for my candle to make it last longer?

Trim the wick to 1/4 inch before every burn.

Always let the wax melt across the surface fully with every burn.

Avoid burning for more than 3-4 hours at a time.

Keep away from fans, open windows, and drafts to prevent uneven burning.

Never leave a burning candle unattended.

Can I reuse the candle jar once it's finished?
Absolutely. Once the candle is done, clean out the leftover wax by placing the jar in warm water or freezing it to pop out the wax. The jar can then be reused as a planter, pen holder, or for storing little keepsakes.
Why does my candle jar blacken when it's low on wax? How can I avoid soot while burning my candle?

This can happen when the wick isn't trimmed regularly or if the flame is too close to the glass. Always keep the wick trimmed to 1/4 inch, and discontinue use when about 1/2 inch of wax remains to avoid overheating the jar.

When the wick is not trimmed properly, it mushrooms and creates more soot, which can easily be avoided by trimming it. You can use your fingers, only on a cooled-down wick to avoid any burns, or use wick trimmers.

Are your candles safe for pets or people with allergies?
Our candles are made using clean-burning soy wax and premium quality fragrance oils. However, if you or your pet have sensitivities, we recommend consulting a doctor or vet. Always burn in a well-ventilated room.
