Siesha - A Fragrance of Connection, Memories, and Rituals.

Siesha was born from a deep sense of connection - to the past, to the present, and to the people and moments that shape us.

Inspired by the founder's grandmother, who found meaning in every ritual, Siesha is a tribute to the way we connect - with ourselves, with faith, and with each other.

Where moments become memories

Rooted in Indian heritage yet designed for modern life, Siesha is about slowing down, cherishing small moments, and finding warmth in everyday rituals. A familiar fragrance can transport us to temple visits with grandparents, to childhood outings, to quiet moments of reflection.

`

That is the essence of Siesha - bringing back the beauty of connections in a way that feels effortless and meaningful today.

Siesha is for those who seek deeper connections, lasting memories, and the quiet strength of rituals - one flicker at a time.
