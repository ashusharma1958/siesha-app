Siesha – A Fragrance of Connection, Memories & Rituals

Siesha was born from a simple belief, the most meaningful moments in life are often the quietest ones.

Rooted in Indian heritage and crafted for modern living, every Siesha fragrance is designed to evoke a feeling. A familiar scent can take you back to temple visits with your grandparents, carefree childhood afternoons, festive celebrations, or a moment of stillness after a long day.

Where moments become memories

Inspired by the founder's grandmother, who found beauty and purpose in everyday rituals. Siesha is a tribute to the connections that shape us, the memories that we carry through life. For my grandmother, rituals were never just routines. They were acts of presence, gratitude, and care. That philosophy continues to inspire everything we create.

At Siesha, we believe fragrance is more than something you smell—it's something you feel. It has the power to transform spaces, awaken memories, and turn everyday moments into meaningful rituals.

Siesha is for those who value connection, cherish nostalgia, and believe that the smallest rituals often leave the deepest impressions—one candle, one fragrance, one flicker at a time.

Meet the Friends Turned Founders

Siesha was founded by two friends who shared one dream—to build something meaningful together. What began as endless conversations and big ideas slowly grew into a brand rooted in creativity, purpose, and a shared belief in thoughtful craftsmanship.

Together, we created Siesha to celebrate memories, rituals, and the emotions that fragrance can evoke. Every candle reflects our journey, bringing warmth, nostalgia, and meaningful moments into everyday life.
