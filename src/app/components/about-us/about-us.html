Handcrafted Scented Candles & Home

Rooted in Indian heritage and crafted for modern living, our luxury scented candles and home fragrances are designed to bring warmth, elegance, and intention into every space.

Each fragrance is thoughtfully created to help you slow down, reconnect, and make everyday moments feel special.

Where moments become memories

Inspired by the founder's grandmother, Siesha is a celebration of the rituals that bring meaning to everyday life. For her, simple moments were acts of presence, gratitude, and care—a philosophy that continues to guide everything we create.

We believe fragrance is more than a scent; it's an experience. A familiar aroma can awaken cherished memories, create comfort, and transform ordinary moments into meaningful rituals.

OUR STORY

Siesha was founded by two friends, Abhinav and Mehak, who shared a dream of building something meaningful together. In 2025, what began as passionate conversations grew into a brand rooted in creativity, purpose, and thoughtful craftsmanship.

Through handcrafted scented candles and home fragrances, we celebrate memories, rituals, and the emotions that fragrance can evoke — bringing warmth, comfort, and connection into everyday life.
